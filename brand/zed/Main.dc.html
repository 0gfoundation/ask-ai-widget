Rules
Head, two ears, one visor. Nothing else. No antenna, collar or body. Anything below the helmet breaks under 24px.
Ears are the same glass as the visor, drawn behind the head so only the domes show.
Outline scales with size. About one device pixel at 16px, two at 48px and above.
Eyes do all the expression. Idle, thinking, answered, error, resting. Nothing else on the character moves.
Eyes are {{eyeW}} wide by {{eyeH}} tall in a 120-unit frame. At 32px and under they grow by one unit in width and two in height.
